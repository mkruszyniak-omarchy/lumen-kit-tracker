# Authors & acknowledgements

## Lumen KIT

**KRUChY & Lumen**

- **[KRUChY](https://github.com/mkruszyniak-omarchy)** — concept,
  musical direction, workflow decisions and hands-on testing on the Tracker Mini.
- **Lumen** — AI engineering collaborator: DSP implementation, firmware
  integration, automated checks and documentation, developed in collaboration
  with KRUChY.

Version **0.1** is a proof of concept: an experiment in what a custom percussion
engine can do inside the Polyend Tracker Community Edition firmware.
